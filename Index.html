02 / RANGE

What we build

Standard configurations below — most units are adapted to your voltage, wattage, and mounting before they ship.

HP-STR

Strip Heaters

Flat, high-density heating for mould walls, platens, and flat surfaces requiring even heat distribution.

HP-TUB

Tubular Heaters

Bent to shape for tanks, ovens, and air-heating applications where element geometry matters.

HP-CRT

Cartridge Heaters

Compact, high-watt-density inserts for die, mould, and hot-runner heating in tight bore sizes.

HP-BND

Band & Nozzle Heaters

Clamp-on heating for barrels and nozzles on extrusion and injection-moulding lines.

HP-IMM

Immersion Heaters

Direct-liquid heating elements for tanks, baths, and process fluids, built to your flange spec.

HP-PNL

Control Panels

Thermostats, controllers, and switchgear built alongside the heater, sized to the load.
